FAQs

Questions answered.

What is StackUp Nest?
+
StackUp Nest is a savings and investment circle combined with a trading and commerce group, all operating out of Ghana. Members save monthly, access loans, and benefit from our growing trading division — all managed through a secure digital portal.
How do I join the group?
+
Fill out our online registration form with your name, WhatsApp number and email. Your application is reviewed by the admin and your account is activated once approved. You'll receive a welcome email with your Member ID and portal access details.
How does the loan system work?
+
Active members in good standing can apply for a loan of up to 80% of their total contributions directly through the Member Portal. Applications are reviewed by the admin typically within 24 hours. Repayments are tracked automatically and you'll receive alerts as your due date approaches.
What happens at year-end cashout?
+
At the end of each year, every active member receives a payout calculated as their total yearly contributions minus any outstanding loan balance and unpaid penalties. The admin previews the cashout first, then distributes to members once confirmed.
What is the Trading & Commerce division?
+
Our trading arm handles import and export, wholesale distribution, commodities trading and professional advisory services for consumers across Ghana. Members get priority access to trade deals and can participate in commerce opportunities facilitated through StackUp Nest.
Is my data and money safe?
+
All member data is stored securely and only accessible to you through your personal portal and the group admin. Every transaction is recorded with full transparency. Your savings and loan records are always visible to you in real time.
