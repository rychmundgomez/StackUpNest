FAQs

Questions,
answered.

What exactly is StackUp Nest?
+
StackUp Nest is Ghana's premier savings and investment circle combined with a trading and commerce group. Members save monthly, access loans, and benefit from our growing trading division — all managed through a secure digital Member Portal backed by real-time records.
How do I become a member?
+
Complete the online registration form with your name, WhatsApp number, and email. Your application is reviewed by the admin and your account is activated upon approval — typically within 24 hours. You'll receive a welcome email with your Member ID and portal access.
How does the loan system work?
+
Active members in good standing can apply for a loan of up to 80% of their total contributions directly through the Member Portal. Applications are reviewed within 24 hours. Repayments are tracked automatically and you receive alerts as your due date approaches.
What happens at year-end cashout?
+
At the end of each cycle, every active member receives a payout equal to their total yearly contributions minus any outstanding loan balance and unpaid penalties. The admin previews and confirms before disbursement — transparent from calculation to transfer.
Is my money and data secure?
+
All member data is stored securely and accessible only to you and the group admin. Every transaction is logged with full transparency. Your savings and loan records are always visible to you in real time through your personal portal.
What is the Trading & Commerce division?
+
Our trading arm handles import and export, wholesale distribution, commodities trading, and professional advisory services across Ghana. Members get priority access to trade deals and can participate in commerce opportunities facilitated through StackUp Nest.
